Privacy

Owner review required before publication. This draft describes the current static website. Nicole must confirm it matches the farm's actual inquiry and enrollment practices and the selected website host's data practices.

Information on this website

This website has no inquiry form, account registration, analytics, advertising, or tracking scripts. It does not ask visitors to submit personal information. The website provides links that let a parent or guardian open their messaging or email application to contact Nicole. Those applications and services are operated by third parties and have their own privacy practices.

Parent and guardian inquiries

Parents and guardians should contact Nicole about the program. Please do not include sensitive information about a child in an initial text or email. Information shared directly with the business is handled outside this website; ask Nicole how it is used, stored, and deleted.

Website hosting

The website host may process technical information such as IP addresses and request logs to deliver and secure the site. The host, retention periods, and security settings depend on the eventual publishing provider and must be confirmed before launch. The production site should use HTTPS.

Children's information

This website is intended for adults seeking program information. It does not knowingly collect information directly from children. The business owner should review applicable privacy obligations and obtain appropriate parent or guardian permissions for any information collected during enrollment.
