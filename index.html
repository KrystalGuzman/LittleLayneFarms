About the program

Little Layne Farms is a hands-on enrichment program taught by an experienced elementary school art teacher, professional fine-art photographer, and passionate hobby farmer. Children have opportunities to learn, explore, and grow through meaningful experiences with animals, gardens, open space, and creative activities.

Helpful details

Frequently asked questions

What ages or grade levels can participate?

The enrichment program is listed for kindergarten through 4th grade (K–4).

Where is Little Layne Farms?

Little Layne Farms is located in Escondido, California, in San Diego County. Contact Nicole for the exact address and visit details.

What days are classes offered, and what are the hours?

Tuesday and Friday sessions are listed. Both run from 9:00 AM to 12:00 PM.

How much does each session cost?

Each listed session costs $500 for eight weeks. A multi-day discount is available; contact Nicole for details.

How long is each program?

Each listed Tuesday or Friday program is an eight-week session.

What do children do at the farm?

Children take part in creative activities and explore chickens, mini horses, a mini pig, a bunny, cats, a vegetable garden, and open outdoor spaces.

Is a multi-day discount available?

A multi-day discount is available. Contact Nicole for current pricing details.

Are the December session dates confirmed?

Two sessions are announced as starting in December, but exact class dates are not listed. Contact Nicole for current dates and availability.

How can I ask about enrollment?

Parents and guardians can text Nicole at 760-566-6094 or email nicolelittlelayne@gmail.com to ask about availability, enrollment, and multi-day pricing.
